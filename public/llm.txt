## About Udyamita
Udyamita Global LLP is a business growth and transformation partner for local businesses in India. It is based in Pune, Maharashtra. Udyamita combines consulting, digital growth, technology, and automation into one practical growth system.

## Core positioning
- Name: Udyamita Global LLP
- Type: Business Growth and Transformation Partner
- Location: Pune, Maharashtra, India
- Mission: Help small businesses get discovered, win customers, and run better, using a simple stack of digital services that pay for themselves.
- Vision: Become the default growth partner for local businesses in Maharashtra, and then India.
- Slogan: Get Discovered. Win Customers. Run Better.

## What Udyamita does
Udyamita helps businesses get discovered, win customers, and run better. It begins with diagnosis (not service-selling), implements solutions, measures results, and optimizes over time. The central idea: treat the service as the product and the cooperative network as the moat.

## The Growth OS (10 stages)
1. Diagnose - Find what is actually broken before spending a rupee.
2. Discover - Understand how customers find (or fail to find) the business.
3. Quantify - Turn intuition into numbers worth discussing.
4. Strategize - Choose the few moves that matter most.
5. Prioritize - Fix what hurts most, first.
6. Implement - Ship the work, with delivery boundaries.
7. Measure - Prove the number moved.
8. Optimize - Cut what doesn't pay for itself.
9. Scale - Repeat what works, without breaking operations.
10. Compound - Stack improvements so each one lifts the next.

## The Business Health Check
A free diagnostic tool that scores a business across five dimensions: Discoverability, Trust, Lead Flow, Follow-up, and Systems. Nine questions, two minutes. Produces a score out of 100, identifies the largest growth leak, and recommends the single move worth making first.
